**Campaigns → New campaign** (Super Admins, Accounts Managers, Recruiters):
1. **Recipients**: select candidates.
2. **Email content**: **Subject line** and **Email body** (required). Start from a template (Outreach, Interview Invite, Offer Update, Follow-up) and use **Insert variable…** for first name, skills, job title and more. A live preview is shown.
3. **Settings**: **Track opens** and **Track clicks**.
4. **Review**: **Review & send campaign**, or **Save as draft**.

Campaigns are sent straight away; scheduling for later isn't available yet. Your mailbox must be connected.

**Results:** click **Analytics** on a sent campaign for delivered, open rate, click rate, unsubscribes, top links and per-recipient status. Unsubscribed people can be re-subscribed from the **Unsubscribes** tab.
