The LinkedIn agent works in your own browser, signed in to your own LinkedIn, through the **ZorHire Assistant** extension or the ZorHire Windows app. ZorHire never asks for your LinkedIn password.

**Start a campaign:**
1. **LinkedIn agent → New campaign**, or **Source on LinkedIn** on a job.
2. The AI writes the LinkedIn search (or a Recruiter Lite Boolean and filters) from the job. Adjust it and start the campaign.
3. The assistant runs the search and imports people. The AI scores each one and writes a short personal connection note.

**Review:** open the campaign. Under **To review**, **Approve** or **Skip** each person, **Edit note** or **Rewrite** it, or use **Approve all scoring 70+**. Approved invitations go out from your account a few minutes apart, in working hours. People who accept become candidates in the job as **Sourced**.

**Safety limits:** at most 40 a day and 100 a week per recruiter, never the same person twice, and the agent pauses itself if LinkedIn shows a warning.

**Recruiter Lite:** the AI drafts InMail subjects and messages; you send them in Recruiter Lite.

Each person scored with a note counts toward the **LinkedIn agent** AI allowance.
