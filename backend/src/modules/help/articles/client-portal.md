After signing in you'll see **Open positions**: each job shared with you, with location, experience, skills and how many candidates are **awaiting review**.

**Review candidates:**
1. Click **View candidates** on a job.
2. Under **Awaiting review**, read each candidate's summary and skills, and **View resume** if available.
3. Click **Approve**, **Hold** or **Reject**, add optional **Notes**, and click **Confirm**.

Reviewed candidates move to **Already reviewed** with your decision; click **Change** to update it. Your recruitment partner sees your decision and notes straight away.

If you see no positions, your recruitment partner hasn't shared any yet.
