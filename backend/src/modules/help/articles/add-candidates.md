**One candidate:** **Candidates → Add candidate**.
1. Optionally choose the resume in **Resume (PDF/DOC/DOCX)**. ZorHire fills in the form; check anything tagged **Needs review**.
2. Fill **First name** and **Last name** (required) and at least an **email or phone number**.
3. Recruiters and Vendor Managers must pick **Add to job**.
4. Click **Create profile**.

**Many candidates:** **Candidates → Bulk upload**.
1. Drop the resumes (PDF, DOC, DOCX) into the box.
2. Optionally pick **Assign to job**; otherwise they go to the candidate pool.
3. When files show **Ready**, click **Preview**, check each person's details, then **Save N candidates**. The resume file is attached automatically.

**Drag and drop anywhere:** drag PDF or Word resumes from your desktop or Outlook onto any ZorHire page. You'll see "Drop resumes to add candidates" and the bulk upload opens with the files.

**From LinkedIn or Naukri:** use **Save to ZorHire** in the ZorHire Assistant extension or the Windows app.

Vendors can also submit candidates through the vendor portal.
