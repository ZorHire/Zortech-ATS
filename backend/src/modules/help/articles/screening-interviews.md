The screening interview is a short written chat (about 10 minutes) that the candidate opens from an email link, at any time. The AI asks about their experience, notice period, salary expectations and hands-on skills, and follows up on vague answers.

**Send one:** on a pipeline card click **Send screening invite** → **Send invite**, or in the candidate window's **AI insights** tab click **Send screening interview**. The candidate needs an email address, and a mailbox must be connected.

**Automatically:** turn on **Autopilot** with **Auto screen** on the job; shortlisted candidates get the invite.

**Read the result:** candidate window → **AI insights** → **Screening interview** shows the status, **Screening score**, a **Recommendation** (Advance / Review / Do not advance), a summary, competency scores, logistics (notice period, expected CTC, location, work mode), red flags and the full transcript.

Each interview sent counts toward your company's monthly **AI screening interviews** allowance. An interview a candidate has started always finishes.

If you see "Screening chat is not yet enabled", the feature hasn't been switched on for your company; contact ZorTech support.
