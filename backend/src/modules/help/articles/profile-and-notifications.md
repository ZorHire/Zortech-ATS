**My Profile:** click your name in the top bar.
- **Add photo / Change photo**: JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB.
- Edit **Full name**, **Phone** and **Department**, then **Save Changes**.
- Your email and role can't be changed here; an admin changes roles.
- To change your password, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, or ask an admin to reset it.

**Notifications:** the bell in the top bar shows how many are unread. Click it to see them, click one to mark it read, or **Mark all read**. Notifications cover things like new JD assignments, interviews, candidates moving stages and vendor submissions. Admins also get an alert when the company reaches 80% and 100% of a monthly AI allowance.

The ZorHire Windows app shows the same notifications as desktop alerts.
