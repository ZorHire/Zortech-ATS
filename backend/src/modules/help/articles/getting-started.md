ZorHire is organised in the left menu. You only see the items your role allows.

| Menu group | Items | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Work | **Dashboard**, **Jobs**, **Interviews**, **Offers**, **Campaigns**, **AI agents**, **LinkedIn agent** | Day-to-day hiring |
| Talent | **Candidates**, **Resume search** | Your candidate database |
| Partners | **Clients**, **Vendors**, **Vendor leaderboard**, **JD assignments** | Clients you hire for and staffing vendors |
| Insights | **Analytics**, **Reports** | How hiring is going |
| Settings | **Admin**, **Email settings**, **WhatsApp**, **AI usage**, **Job boards**, **Subscription**, **Help** | Setup and help |

**Top bar:** **Search…** (or press **Ctrl K**) finds candidates, jobs, clients and pages. **Help** opens Aruna, the help assistant. The bell shows your notifications. Your name opens **My Profile**.

**A typical flow:**
1. Connect your mailbox in **Email settings** (needed before ZorHire can send any email for you).
2. Create a job (**Jobs → Create job**), optionally from the client's JD file.
3. Add candidates (**Candidates → Bulk upload** or **Add candidate**), or let vendors submit them.
4. Work the job's **Pipeline**: shortlist, screen, submit to the client, interview, offer.
5. Optionally switch on **Autopilot** on the job so AI agents shortlist, screen and schedule for you.

Admins should also invite the team from **Admin → Invite user**.
