**Subscription** (Super Admins, Accounts Managers) shows your plan, price per user per month, renewal date and payment history.

**Plans** (priced per user per month; yearly billing is cheaper):
- **Starter**: 5–25 users.
- **Growth**: 26–150 users; adds AI resume screening and ranking, interview scheduling automation, advanced analytics and API access.
- **Enterprise**: 150+ users; contact sales.

**Upgrade:** **View Plans** → **Upgrade** or **Get Started**, then pay in the Razorpay checkout. For Enterprise, click **Contact Sales**.

**Cancel:** **Cancel subscription**. Your team keeps access until the end of the paid period, and you can reactivate later.

Without an active plan, ZorHire only shows the pricing and subscription pages. For the AI actions included in each plan, see **AI usage**.
