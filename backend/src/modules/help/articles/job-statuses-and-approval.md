**Statuses:** Draft → Pending review → Active → On hold / Filled / Cancelled / Expired.

**Approval flow** (on the job page):
1. While a job is **Draft**, anyone on staff can click **Submit for review**. It becomes **Pending review**.
2. A **Super Admin** or **Accounts Manager** clicks **Approve** (the job becomes **Active** and assigned vendors are told) or **Reject** (back to **Draft** for changes). A note is optional.
3. Each submission is kept under **Version History**.

Admins see "N JDs awaiting your approval" at the top of **Jobs**.

**Changing status directly:** Super Admins and Accounts Managers can use the status dropdown in the Jobs list; on the job page, Recruiters can change it too. Choosing **Filled** or **Cancelled** asks you to confirm, because it stops hiring on the job.

Only **Active** jobs appear when you add candidates to a job.

Editing a job's title, skills or description after creating it isn't available yet.
