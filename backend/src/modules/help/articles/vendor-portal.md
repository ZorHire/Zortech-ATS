After signing in you'll see **Assigned jobs** and **My submissions** in the left menu.

**Assigned jobs:** the jobs your company has been asked to source for, with client, location, experience, skills, openings, how many you've submitted and the **Submission deadline**. Search by title, location or client.

**Submit a candidate:**
1. Click **Submit candidate** on the job.
2. Enter **Full name** and **Email** (required), plus phone, experience, skills and a cover note.
3. Click **Submit candidate**.

**My submissions:** every candidate you've submitted, the job, and their status (the hiring stage, or **Pending**). If a candidate was disqualified, the reason is shown. Totals show how many are under review, shortlisted and placed.

If you have no jobs, your recruitment partner hasn't assigned any yet; contact them.
