Needs the ZorHire Assistant extension or the ZorHire Windows app.

1. Open the job and click **Post free**.
2. Tick the boards (LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Naukri free job posting, Unstop, HireMee, Internshala, apna, Cutshort, Wellfound). Boards meant for freshers aren't pre-selected for experienced roles.
3. Click the button to open them. Each board opens in a tab with a ZorHire panel.
4. Sign in or create a free account on the board yourself, open its post-a-job form, and click **Fill form**. It fills the title, company, location, experience, salary, skills, description and apply email. It never enters passwords and never clicks Post.
5. Check the form, post it, then click **I've posted it** (or **Skip board**).

For job boards connected by API, use **Publish to boards** instead.
