title: Assign JDs to vendors and recruiters
category: Vendors and partners
audience: staff
summary: Share a job with vendors or recruiters, with a deadline.
keywords: assign jd, send to vendors, share job, deadline, jd assignments, recruiter assignment
order: 20
---

1. On the job page click **Send to vendors**, or on **Vendors** click **Assign JD**.
2. Super Admins and Accounts Managers can switch between **Vendor** and **Recruiter**; Vendor Managers assign to vendors only.
3. Choose the JD in **Select JD**, select the vendors (or recruiters) and a **Deadline** (1–7 days).
4. Click **Assign**. Each one gets an email from your mailbox, so connect it in **Email settings** first.

Vendors then see the job in their portal and can submit candidates. Recruiters see it under **Your Jobs**.

**JD assignments** lists who each job is assigned to; click × on a name to remove the assignment.
