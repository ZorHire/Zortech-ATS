Open a job and click **Pipeline**. Each column is a stage:

New → Sourced → Screened → Shortlisted → Submitted to client → Interview scheduled → Interview completed → Selected → Offer extended → Offer accepted / Offer declined → Joined, plus **Disqualified**.

**Move a candidate:** drag the card by its grip handle to another column, or change the stage dropdown on the card. Click a card to open the candidate.

**Disqualify:** move the card to **Disqualified** (or **Offer declined**), add an optional **Reason** such as "notice period too long", and confirm. The reason is saved on the candidate, and vendors see it on their submissions.

**On each card:** AI scores, key skills, **Client profile** (write a profile to send to the client) and **Send screening invite**.

**Run AI shortlisting** (top right) scores everyone eligible against the job and moves strong matches to Shortlisted.

Moving candidates to **Offer extended**, **Offer accepted** or **Joined** is how offers and placements are recorded; they then appear in **Offers**.
