The ZorHire Assistant powers the LinkedIn agent, **Save to ZorHire** and **Post free**. If you use the ZorHire Windows app, it's already built in, so skip this.

1. Go to **LinkedIn agent** and download the ZorHire Assistant. Unzip it.
2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions, switch on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder.
3. Back in ZorHire, create a connection code on the **LinkedIn agent** page and paste it into the extension.
4. Stay signed in to LinkedIn in that Chrome.

The **Your LinkedIn connection** card then shows your LinkedIn name, Premium and Recruiter Lite, and today's invitations. Under **Connected browsers and apps** you can disconnect a browser.
