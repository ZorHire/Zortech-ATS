Autopilot is switched on per job, on the job page.

**What it does:**
1. **Shortlists** new candidates: scores each against the job (0–100) and moves those at or above the **Shortlist threshold** to Shortlisted.
2. **Screens** them (with **Auto screen** on): emails a link to the AI screening interview.
3. **Schedules** (with **Auto schedule** on): candidates who score at or above the **Screening pass score** get a link to pick one of your **Interview slots**.

**Settings on the job:** the two thresholds, **Auto screen**, **Auto schedule**, **Interview duration**, **Daily outreach cap** (emails per day for the job) and **Interview slots**. Click **Save settings**.

**Safety:** Autopilot never rejects anyone. Low scores are marked **Needs review** for a recruiter. Emails go from the company mailbox (or the job recruiter's), so set one up in **Email settings**.

**See what happened:** **AI agents** shows today's numbers and a log of every step. **Pause all agents** stops Autopilot for the whole company.

Autopilot uses your company's monthly AI allowances. When one runs out it waits, and carries on after the 1st or a top-up.
