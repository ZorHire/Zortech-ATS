**Vendors** is for Super Admins, Accounts Managers and Vendor Managers.

**Add a vendor:** **Vendors → Add vendor**. Optionally upload a vendor document to auto-fill, then fill company, contact, specialisations, geographies, **Vendor tier** (Preferred, Standard, Blocked), registration number and GST ID, and click **Add vendor**. Many at once: **Bulk import**.

**Onboarding documents:** open the vendor → **Documents → Upload** (NDA, Service agreement, Insurance or Other; PDF, Word or image up to 10 MB; optional expiry date). A vendor shows **Onboarded** once an active NDA and Service agreement are both on file; otherwise **Docs pending** or **Expired**. Only Super Admins and Accounts Managers can revoke or delete documents.

**Performance:** the table shows submissions, shortlist rate, fill rate, quality score and SLA. **Vendor leaderboard** ranks vendors by quality. In a vendor's window you can rate recent submissions (1–5 stars) and record **Contracts** (MSA, NDA, SOW).

Tiers are internal; vendors never see them. Blocked or inactive vendors can't be assigned JDs.
