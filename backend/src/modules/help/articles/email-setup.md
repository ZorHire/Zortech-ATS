ZorHire sends emails from your own company mailbox, so connect it first.

**Your mailbox:** **Email settings**.
1. Choose your provider: Zoho Mail, Zoho Mail India, Zoho Mail Europe or Google Workspace.
2. Create an **App Password** (the page shows the steps for Zoho and Google). Use the App Password, not your normal password. Only company-domain addresses work, and for Zoho, SMTP access must be on.
3. Enter your email and the app password, click **Connect email**, then **Test connection**.

**Company mailbox** (Super Admins and Accounts Managers): one shared mailbox for system and AI emails, such as screening invites, scheduling links and password resets. Fill **Mailbox address**, sender name, provider and app password, click **Connect company mailbox**, then **Send test email**.

**Common errors:**
- "Email is not configured": connect your mailbox.
- "Invalid email credentials": create a new app password and reconnect.
- "SMTP access is disabled on your Zoho account": enable it in Zoho Mail → Settings → Security.
- "Email sending limit reached": wait and try later.
