**Schedule an interview:**
1. Open the candidate (from **Candidates** or a pipeline card).
2. On **Overview**, click **Schedule interview**.
3. Pick the date and time, the type (Video Call, Phone or In-Person), the duration, and optionally the interviewer's name and meeting link.
4. Click **Send invite & save**. The invite is emailed from your mailbox and the interview is saved.

The candidate must be in a job's pipeline for the interview to be saved, and needs an email address. Scheduling doesn't move their stage; move the card yourself.

**Let candidates pick a slot:** with **Autopilot → Auto schedule** (or **Send scheduling link** in **AI insights**), candidates who pass screening get a link to choose one of the job's **Interview slots**, and get a calendar file.

**Interviews page:** see **Upcoming**, **Today**, **Past** and **All**. Change a status (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, No show) with **Change…**. Click **+ Add feedback** to record a **Score (0–10)** and notes.

There isn't an interview question generator in ZorHire yet.
