Your plan includes a number of AI actions each month, shared by your whole team. See them in **Settings → AI usage**.

| Allowance | One action is |
|---|---|
| AI shortlisting | one candidate assessed against one job |
| AI screening interviews | one screening interview sent (including its evaluation) |
| AI client profiles | one profile written for a client |
| LinkedIn agent | one LinkedIn person scored with a personal note |

- Re-running the same candidate, profile or person in the same month is free.
- Per user per month: **Growth** includes 150 / 30 / 20 / 300 of the above; **Enterprise** 200 / 40 / 30 / 400; **Starter** has no AI shortlisting or screening, and includes 10 client profiles and 100 LinkedIn people. A free trial has a fixed pool for the whole company.
- Counts reset on the 1st of each month.

**When an allowance runs out:** those AI actions pause until the 1st; everything else keeps working. Autopilot picks up where it left off later, and LinkedIn people still get a keyword score and template note. Admins get an alert at 80% and at 100%.

**Need more?** Upgrade the plan in **Subscription**, or ask ZorTech for an add-on pack.

Reading resumes and JDs, the pipeline's **Run AI shortlisting**, search, and this Help assistant don't count.
