**Vendor portal users** (admins only):
1. Go to **Admin → Invite user**.
2. Enter their name and email, choose the role **Vendor**, and pick the **Linked vendor company**.
3. Set a temporary password and click **Send invite**. They change it at first sign-in.

They'll see the jobs assigned to their company and can submit candidates and track them.

**Client portal users** see the candidates submitted for their jobs and approve, hold or reject them with notes. They can't be invited from the Admin page yet; contact ZorTech support to set up a client user and choose which jobs they see.
