Only **Super Admins** and **Accounts Managers** can create jobs.

**One job:**
1. Go to **Jobs** and click **Create job**.
2. To save typing, use **Auto-fill from JD file** and choose the client's JD (PDF, DOC or DOCX). ZorHire reads it and fills the form. Fields it isn't sure about are highlighted with a **needs review** tag.
3. Check or fill **Job title** and **Client** (both required), department, location, work mode, experience range, priority, **Mandatory skills** and **Preferred skills** (comma-separated) and the job description.
4. Click **Post job**.

The full form (press **Ctrl K** → **New job**) also has **Status**, **Budget min/max (₹)** and **Headcount**, and accepts TXT files in **Upload a JD to auto-fill**.

**Many jobs at once:** **Jobs → Bulk upload**, drop several JD files, wait until each shows **Ready**, click **Preview**, check the details, then **Create N jobs**. Bulk-created jobs are saved as **Draft**.

**Tips**
- If auto-fill can't read the file you'll see "Could not extract data, please fill manually."
- The client must exist first; add it with **Add client** on the Jobs page.
- After creating, open the job to send it to vendors, publish it, or switch on Autopilot.
