Open a job from **Jobs**. The buttons at the top:

- **Pipeline (n)**: the job's candidate board.
- **Send to vendors**: share the JD with vendors (Super Admin, Accounts Manager, Vendor Manager).
- **Source on LinkedIn**: start a LinkedIn agent campaign for this job.
- **Post free**: post to free job boards with the ZorHire Assistant.
- **Publish to boards**: post to paid job boards your company has connected (tick boards → **Publish to N boards**). If none are connected you'll be pointed to **Settings → Job boards**.
- **Add candidate**: add someone straight into this job.

Further down:
- **Autopilot**: let AI agents shortlist, screen and schedule for this job.
- **Sourcing — Boolean search**: ready-made search strings for LinkedIn (Google X-ray) and for Naukri, Indeed, Monster and Glassdoor, each with **Copy**.
- **Matching candidates → Find matching candidates**: finds people already in your database who fit, with a % match. Use **Add to pipeline** or **Add all** (they join as **Shortlisted**).
- **Job board distribution**: where the job is live and how many applications came from boards.
- **Approval workflow** and **Version History**.
