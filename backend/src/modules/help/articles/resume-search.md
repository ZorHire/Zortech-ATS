Go to **Resume search**.
1. Enter **Keywords** and/or **Skills** (press Enter after each skill), and filter by **Current role**, **Experience**, **Location**, **Notice period** and **Source**.
2. Click **Search candidates**.

**Results:** the best fits (75%+ match) appear under **Top matches**; everyone else is in the table with a match %. Sort by relevance, last updated or experience. Click a row to preview the person, with matching skills highlighted, then **Email**, **Add to job** or **Open profile**.

**Bulk actions:** tick people to **Email** them together or **Add to pipeline**. **Export CSV** downloads the whole result.

**Save a search:** after searching, click **Save search** and give it a name. Find it later in **Saved searches** and click **Run**.

To find people for a specific job, the job page's **Find matching candidates** is quicker.
