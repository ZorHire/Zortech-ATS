**Sign in:** on the **Sign in to ZorHire** page enter your **Email address** and **Password**, or use **Continue with Google** / **Continue with Microsoft**. Single sign-on only works if your account was already created with that email.

**Forgot your password:**
1. Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your email and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email within 15 minutes and set a new password (at least 8 characters).

**First sign-in:** if you were invited with a temporary password, ZorHire asks you to change it straight away (**Update Password**).

An admin can also set a new temporary password for you.

**Can't sign in?**
- "Incorrect email or password": check the email, or use **Forgot password?**.
- "Your session expired": just sign in again.
- "Can't reach ZorHire right now": check your internet and click **Try again**.
- Your account may have been deactivated; ask your admin.
