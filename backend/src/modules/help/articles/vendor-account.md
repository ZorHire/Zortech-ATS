- Your account is created by the company you supply candidates to. You'll get a temporary password and must change it at first sign-in.
- Forgot it? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- To add colleagues from your company, ask your contact at that company; vendors can't invite users themselves.
- You only see jobs assigned to your company and your own submissions.
