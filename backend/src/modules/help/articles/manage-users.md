**Admin** is for Super Admins and Accounts Managers.

**Invite someone:** **Invite user** → **Full name**, **Email address**, **Role** (Super Admin, Accounts Manager, Vendor Manager, Recruiter or Vendor) and a **Temporary password** → **Send invite**. For a Vendor, also pick the **Linked vendor company**. They must change the password at first sign-in.

If the invite email fails, the user is still created; connect your mailbox in **Email settings** and give them the temporary password yourself.

**On each user's row:**
- **Reset password**: set a new temporary password.
- **Deactivate / Activate**: block or restore access without deleting.
- **Delete user**: permanent.

**Pending reset** counts users who haven't changed their temporary password yet.
