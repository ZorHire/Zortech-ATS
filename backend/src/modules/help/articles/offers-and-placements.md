**Offers** is a read-only view built from the pipeline. To record an offer or a joiner, move the candidate in the job's pipeline:
- **Offer extended** shows as **Pending**
- **Offer accepted** shows as **Accepted** (and in **Placements**)
- **Offer declined** shows as **Declined** (you can add a reason)
- **Joined** shows in **Placements**

The **Offers** tab lists each offer with job, client, current → expected CTC and date; **Placements** lists accepted and joined candidates.

Offer letters and offer approvals aren't part of ZorHire yet.
