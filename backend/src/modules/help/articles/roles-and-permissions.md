Your role is shown under your name in the top bar. Admins set roles in **Admin**.

| Role (Admin page name) | Shown in top bar as | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Admin | Everything: jobs, clients, approvals, vendors, reports, users, subscription, company mailbox. Only Super Admins see **Onboarding**. |
| **Accounts Manager** | Accounts manager | Same as Super Admin except Onboarding. |
| **Recruiter** | Recruiter | **Your Jobs** (jobs assigned to them), candidates, pipeline, **Interviews**, **Offers**, **Campaigns**, their own **Email settings**, WhatsApp, AI usage. Can't see Vendors, Analytics, Reports or Admin. |
| **Vendor Manager** | Vendor manager | Assigned jobs, candidates, **Vendors**, **Vendor leaderboard**, **JD assignments** (can assign to vendors only), **Analytics**. Can't see Interviews, Offers, Campaigns or Email settings. |
| **Vendor** | Vendor | The vendor portal only (their company's assigned jobs and submissions). |

- **Client** users use the client portal to review candidates. They can't be invited from the Admin page yet; ask ZorTech to set them up.
- Recruiters and vendor managers must add each new candidate to one of their jobs.
- If you can't find a menu item or button, your role probably doesn't include it. Ask your admin.
