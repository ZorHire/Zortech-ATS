Click a candidate in **Candidates** to open their window. Tabs:
- **Overview**: contact details, summary, skills, current stage, and actions: **Move to stage**, **Open pipeline**, **Add to another job**, **Send email**, **Schedule interview**, plus current and expected CTC and **Delete candidate**.
- **Resume**: view, **Download** or **Full screen**; upload one if missing (PDF, DOCX or DOC up to 8 MB).
- **Activity**: interviews and the pipeline timeline (who moved them and when).
- **AI insights**: AI shortlisting and screening results, with **Run shortlister**, **Send screening interview** and **Send scheduling link**.
- **WhatsApp**: chat with the candidate from your own number.

**Full profile** (… menu → **Full profile**) shows every job they've applied to, with an **AI match %** per job.

**Add to a job:** **Add to job** → search an **Active** job → choose the stage (default **Shortlisted**) → **Add candidate**. People already in that job are skipped.

**Export:** **Export CSV** downloads the filtered list (or ticked rows).

**Email:** **Send email** opens a composer with quick templates (Shortlisted, Interview Invite, Not Selected, Follow-up). Your mailbox must be connected in **Email settings**.

Editing a candidate's details or adding free-text notes isn't available yet.
