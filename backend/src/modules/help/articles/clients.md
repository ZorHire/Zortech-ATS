Only **Super Admins** and **Accounts Managers** can add or delete clients. Everyone on staff can view them in **Clients**.

**Add a client:** **Clients → Add client** (or **Jobs → Add client**). The form has five tabs:
- **Basic Info**: **Client Name** (required), type, industry, size, website, locations and the primary contact (name, designation, email, phone).
- **Stakeholders**: hiring managers and other contacts.
- **Business & Billing**: engagement type, priority, SLA, billing model, currency, markup %, payment terms.
- **Contract & AI**: contract dates, **MSA Signed**, **NDA Signed**, preferred skills, typical roles, interview process.
- **Internal**: account manager, delivery lead, recruiters, tags, notes.

Click **Save client**. The primary contact's email is used when you email a candidate profile to the client.

On **Clients** you can search, filter by industry, **View** a client's details, or delete one (this also removes related records, so be careful). Editing a client isn't available yet.
