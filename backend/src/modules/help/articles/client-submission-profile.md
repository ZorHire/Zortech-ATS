1. On the candidate's pipeline card, click **Client profile**.
2. Click **Create with AI**. ZorHire reads the resume against the job and writes a one-page profile: a headline, why this candidate fits, each required skill rated (strong / some / not shown) with evidence, experience, education, certifications and a short recruiter's note.
3. **Edit** anything. **Hide candidate's email and phone** is on by default, so the client comes back through you. Tick **Show CTC** to show salary.
4. **Print / PDF** for a clean A4 page, or **Email to client**: it's pre-filled with the client's primary contact and sent from your mailbox. Attaching the original resume is optional (it usually contains contact details).

Emailing it moves the candidate to **Submitted to client** unless you untick that option.

Each new profile counts toward the monthly **AI client profiles** allowance; rewriting the same one in the same month is free.
