**Set your number:** **Settings → WhatsApp → My WhatsApp number**. Enter it with the country code and choose how messages are sent:
- **Click to send** (no setup): ZorHire writes the message and opens WhatsApp or WhatsApp Web; you press Send, then confirm "I sent it". Replies stay in WhatsApp.
- **Automatic**: sends straight from ZorHire and shows replies, but needs your company's WhatsApp Business account connected by an admin.

**Message a candidate:** open the candidate → **WhatsApp** tab. Use a quick message (Interested? / Share CV / Interview reminder / Still interested?) or type your own.

In automatic mode, WhatsApp only allows a free-form message within 24 hours of the candidate's last message; otherwise the company's approved template is used.

**Admins:** connect the company's WhatsApp Business account under **Company WhatsApp Business** on the same page.
