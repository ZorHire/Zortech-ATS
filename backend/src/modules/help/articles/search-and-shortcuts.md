Press **Ctrl K** (⌘K on Mac), press **/** when you're not typing in a field, or click **Search…** in the top bar.

- Type at least 2 characters to find **candidates**, plus **jobs**, **clients** and **pages**.
- Before you type, it offers quick **Actions**: **New job**, **Add candidate**, **Resume search**, **Book interview**.
- Use ↑ ↓ to move, **Enter** to open, **Esc** to close.

For detailed candidate searches by skills, experience, location or notice period, use **Resume search** instead.
