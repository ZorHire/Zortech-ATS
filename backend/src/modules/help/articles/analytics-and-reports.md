**Analytics** (Super Admins, Accounts Managers, Vendor Managers): choose a period (30 days to 1 year, or all time) to see placements, active jobs, offer accept rate and pipeline size, the **Hiring funnel**, **Source effectiveness**, **Monthly placements**, **Vendor performance** and **Recruiter productivity**.

**Reports** (Super Admins, Accounts Managers): choose a date range and a **Report type**:
- **Placement report**: placements by month.
- **Recruiter performance**: jobs, sourced, shortlisted, submitted and placements per recruiter.
- **Hiring funnel**: candidates at each stage.

If a report is empty, try a longer date range.

The **Dashboard** gives everyone a daily snapshot: totals, pipeline, funnel, tasks and recent activity.
