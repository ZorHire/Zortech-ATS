Verify current rates, ceiling, and applicability with your CA before submitting to EPFO — this prepares the numbers, it doesn't file the ECR.

Yearly Close & CA Audit Pack

Yearly P&L, position statement, and a full schedule pack to hand to your CA.
The "Balance Sheet" here is a simplified position statement built only from what this ledger tracks — receivables, payables, and estimated retained earnings. It does not include cash/bank balances, fixed assets, share capital, or loans, so it will not balance to a statutory Balance Sheet on its own. Your CA will need to combine this with your bank statements and fixed asset register for the actual filing.
